# Broad research review boundaries

The campaign discovery worker's ten topic documents and implementation map
are a navigation aid. Its coverage reports 79 requested functions, 78 nonempty
exports, 42 semantic reviews and 18 assembly corroborations, with 0xda350
missing. A completed topic queue does not prove complete engine coverage or
a playable campaign. A private supervisor review retains report identities,
bounded findings, corrections and unanswered questions.

The full-pointer inventory lookup comparison agrees with earlier independent
analysis. However, channel lookup presence is distinct from channel lifetime
readiness and receiver acceptance. No matched sender/receiver observation
proves the proposed relevance-to-index-collision failure chain. Repeating
ClientGivenTo for every weapon is therefore not an established repair.

Port-arms deferral is a useful observation target. Clearing native stance bits
and timers is not equivalent to completing the engine's draw/exit transition.
Movement shadow reseeding likewise requires matched protocol histories; a
receive-boundary discontinuity does not identify the defective field. Neither
raw-memory proposal was adopted.

The SDK declares PHYS_Walking as 1. Grounding checks still need to distinguish
legitimate falling/traversal from invalid reacquisition. The retained walking
evaluator's rejection of a route with substantial height changes must not be
described as proof of a fall through the world.

Authored revive setup accepts an already loaded animation set; it does not
load assets. Native temporary-set removal includes reference/time-dependent
cleanup and a PlayersOnly skip, rather than a demonstrated wait for every
animation node. Loading, mutual peer admission and runtime service lifecycle
remain unfinished. See [service](REVIVE-SERVICE.md) and
[animation](REVIVE-ANIMATION.md).

SDK map-change declarations do not establish controller/pawn persistence
across every mission. Similarly, generated DLC metadata and TOC readback do
not isolate the prior corrupt-DLC cause or establish engine acceptance. Keep
actual dialogue, mission travel, saves and installation evidence separate.
